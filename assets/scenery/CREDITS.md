# Scenery credits

Every model in `assets/scenery/` is CC0 or CC-BY. CC-BY models are listed with their author;
CC0 models are listed without an author. Source files live outside the repo
(`$OPEN_SPEED_SOURCE`, default `~/Dev/open-speed/build/source`); `assets/scenery/*.glb`
are generated by `scripts/build-scenery.mjs` and measured by `scripts/build-scenery-catalog.mjs`.

## Generated

- `monkey-head.glb` - the Last Resort landmark the road drives through. Generated from reference
  images drawn for it, so no third-party model is involved and there is nothing to attribute.
  Rebuild: `build-scenery.mjs monkey-head --rotate 0,180,0`, then `ground-scenery.mjs monkey-head`
  for its 30 m, then `bore-tunnel.py` for the 13 x 10 m passage - image-to-3D returns a closed
  shell, so the mouth arrives as a recess and has to be cut through.

Changes: glTF-to-GLB packaging where needed; legacy cliff material converted to metallic/roughness;
canonical Y-up ground-sitting normalization; textures re-encoded to WebP; runtime height scaling.
The bush albedo is multiplied down (`build-scenery.mjs bush --tint 0.16,0.11,0.9` from the
pre-tint source in `$OPEN_SPEED_SOURCE/scenery/bush/bush.glb`) so its lime green sits on the palm
frond hue and a step darker (viewer probe, green-dominant texels: bush 62,74,44 vs palm 61,75,47).
